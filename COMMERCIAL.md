# Commercial licensing

WebEngine offers a commercial licensing option for Monutchee-owned material
covered by the dual-license policy in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

A commercial agreement may permit use in proprietary applications or distribution
of modified covered code without the GPL's corresponding-source obligations,
within the scope expressly agreed by the relevant rights holder.

Business internal use and commercial distribution that comply with GPLv3 do
not require a commercial purchase. The GPL option remains available.

## Requesting an agreement

Contact the Monutchee maintainers through the contact options on
https://github.com/Monutchee. Identify the repository, version, intended use,
distribution model and permissions needed. Do not post confidential details
in public issues.

This document is an invitation to negotiate, not a commercial license grant.
Alternative permissions take effect only when a written agreement is executed
by the relevant rights holder. Scope, fees, support, warranties and any other
terms must be specified in that agreement. Until then, the applicable
open-source licenses govern.

Third-party code and dependencies retain their own licenses. No commercial
agreement under this offer waives obligations for rights the licensor does
not control. Previously granted MIT, BSD or GPL permissions are not revoked.
